## Design

**Style in one line:** warm paper for one experienced practitioner. Cream page, big Fraunces serif headlines, soft peach and lilac colour blocks, plum text, one magenta for buttons and links, a gold ring round the portrait, generous whitespace, and the small rainbow watercolour butterfly badge from the studio's business card, which the palette is lifted from as well. Confident but unhurried; friendly, not precious. It should feel closer to a well-designed independent studio than to a spa, a clinic or a template.

**How it is built:** a page is a stack of rounded colour blocks and hairline lists on the cream paper. Every page opens with its title set straight on the paper under the header (the home and about pages put a block beside it), and every page closes on the plum studio card. Big type carries the hierarchy; there are no eyebrows, ribbons, icons-as-decoration, gradients, drop shadows or stock-photo backgrounds. Use this system for every page, not just the home page.

- **Feel:** one experienced person who has time for you. Calm, confident, a little warm. Not a spa, not a clinic. Plain headings, no eyebrow labels, no ribbons or sticker notes. Italic is reserved for a spoken line set large (`.statement`): the mission from the business card and Mariya's own quote. No italic accent words inside running text.
- **Palette (light):** cream `#FBF4EC` page, peach `#F5E1D2` and lilac `#DDC3DE` colour blocks, blush `#F8ECF1` for a quieter third block, plum `#3B2440` for text and the footer card, `#5E4A63` for secondary text, magenta `#A8155F` for buttons and link rules (`#7E0F47` on hover), gold `#D9A62E` only for the ring round the portrait. Hairlines are plum at 25%. Mapped to semantic tokens in `src/routes/layout.css`: `background`/`foreground` = cream/plum, `primary` = magenta with white text (`primary-hover` for the deeper magenta), `secondary` = peach, `accent` = lilac, `muted` = blush, `muted-foreground` = the soft plum, `ink`/`ink-foreground` = the plum footer card and its peach text, `gold` = the ring, `border` = the hairline. Dark mode turns the paper deep plum with peach text, darkens the peach and lilac blocks to two plums, makes the buttons and link rules a pale pink with plum text, and inverts the footer card to peach with plum text; the gold ring stays. Always use the semantic tokens; never hardcode a hex in a component.
- **Type:** Fraunces (optical-size axis, weight 500, `-0.025em`) for every heading and price (`font-display`), Fraunces italic 400 for the statements, Public Sans for body at 17px/1.55 and for buttons, nav and row titles at 600 (`font-sans`). Fonts ship via `@fontsource-variable` (`fraunces/opsz.css`, `fraunces/opsz-italic.css`, `public-sans`). Utility classes in `layout.css`: `.display-1` (page titles, 96px on desktop), `.display-2` (section titles, 56px), `.display-3` (block titles, 30px), `.statement` / `.statement-sm` (the italic lines), `.price` / `.price-sm` (rates), `.row-title`, `.lead` / `.lead-sm`, `.text-link`.
- **Rhythm:** 20px gutters on phones, 40px from `md`, 64px from `lg`, content 1200px max (`wrap` utility). Section gaps 64px / 112px (`section-gap`). Radii: 28px on colour blocks (`rounded-block`, 24px on phones), 20px on quote blocks (`rounded-lg`), 999px pills on buttons. Block padding is the `block-pad` utility (24 / 48 / 56px). Lists use 1px hairlines (`border-t`/`border-b`), not cards; the three rates sit in a row divided by hairlines (`rates`).
- **Components:** `Button` is a pill in Public Sans 600 with variants `default` (magenta, white text), `secondary` (peach), `outline` (1.5px border in the current text colour, so it inverts on the plum card; fills with the text colour on hover), `contrast` (the card's text colour as a fill, for the button on the plum footer), `ghost`, `link` (a 2px magenta rule under the words; the same look as the `.text-link` class for plain anchors). Header sits straight on the paper: badge, studio name, three links and a small book button; on phones a hamburger opens a full-width cream menu. Footer is the plum rounded card (studio heading, contrast book button, contact line, studio address with directions link, hours, then a hairline row with the badge, license, email, theme toggle and copyright). The logo is the round badge from the business card, inline SVG in `src/lib/assets/logo.svelte` and `favicon.svg`: a white disc in a golden-yellow ring with "LIVINGOOD WELLNESS CENTER" set round it as one continuous clockwise run in deep magenta Times New Roman Bold caps (converted to outlines by the generator, so no font is needed), letters stretched slightly wider than the font draws them, so the only break in the ring sits at the lower left on the butterfly's body axis, and the watercolour butterfly in the middle: a mirrored pair of wings on a thin body, tilted 30 degrees, in the card's proportions (long forewings swept out to the sides with the apex level with the top of the wing, evenly rounded hindwings below with a straight cut from the bottom of each lobe up into the body so a deep V sits between them, wider than tall); each side is one continuous silhouette with scalloped margins, faint veins that all fan out from one point in the middle of the body, and a thin outline in the same translucent ink, so there is no seam between forewing and hindwing. Each side is painted in layers through `<use>`: a forewing gradient along the wing (dark orange tip through yellow, green and aqua into blue on the left; teal through cyan into blue on the right), a hindwing gradient that fades in below it (deep blue on the left; lavender into magenta on the right), then pooled edges, a few pale patches, the veins and the outline. Body and antennae are a fixed dark ink too, so the mark looks the same on every background and in both themes. The logo colours are the one place a hex value is allowed outside `layout.css`, and the only gradients on the site; keep them identical in both files and in `static/og.png`. Gradient ids come from `$props.id()` because the logo renders several times per page. Decorative unless given a `label`.
- **Photos:** Mariya's portrait sits in the gold ring on the peach hero block (`portrait`) or as a 4:5 photo with block corners (home meet section, about intro). Stock photos only, no AI-generated people. Render content photos with `<enhanced:img>` (the `@sveltejs/enhanced-img` plugin is registered in `vite.config.ts`): give `src` the `#lib/...` path and a `sizes` attribute that describes how wide the slot renders, and let the plugin choose the widths; it emits AVIF/WebP/JPEG at each one. Only add `?w=` when the default widths are clearly wrong for a slot. The first portrait on a page keeps `fetchpriority="high"` and eager loading; everything below the fold is lazy. Plain `<img>` with a Vite URL import is only for things that are not photos (the favicon, `og.png`).
- **Shared page pieces** in `src/lib/components`: `page-intro` (title and lead on the paper, with room for buttons), `portrait` (the gold-ringed circle), `service-list` (hairline rows, two columns when there is room), `rates` (the three rates in a hairline row), `seo` (title, description, canonical, Open Graph) and `json-ld` (LocalBusiness, rendered once from the layout). Every page passes its own `title` and `description` to `SEO`. The Open Graph image is `static/og.png` (1200x630, same palette and type).
- Booking, packages and membership always link to `/book`, never straight to MassageBook. The one product link that goes straight out is the LifeWave button on `/services` (`lifewave.url` in `src/lib/config/site.ts`): it is a store, not booking, so it opens in a new tab with `target="_blank" rel="noopener"` and a trailing `ArrowUpRightIcon` (`data-icon="inline-end"`), the same treatment as the MassageBook buttons on `/book`. Keep its Brand Partner disclaimer next to the button.
- All design tokens live in `src/routes/layout.css`. Every colour variable is defined for both light and dark. Make every page responsive and check both themes.
